\section{Results}

\subsection{Fixed seven-crossing shadow}
\label{sec:seven}

The production shadow (the single seven-crossing PD code in
\texttt{config.py}) admits an instructive failure-then-success arc. End-to-end
AlphaZero self-play (job 23623670; 20 iterations, 64 episodes each, 50 MCTS
simulations) reaches only 71.73\% optimal top-policy actions, 71.52\% mean
optimal mass, and 65.27\% value-sign accuracy: certified \textbf{NO}. Exact
supervision of the original graph transformer (job 23748144) stalls at nearly
identical numbers (71.49\% / 71.44\% / 65.42\%: \textbf{NO}), showing the
bottleneck is representational rather than a self-play defect. Ordered-PD
concatenation (job 23760015) and explicit crossing-state attachment (job
23766423) each remain at \textbf{NO}, while a width-256 MLP over the seven
categorical crossing states solves the table in 38~seconds (job 23775537:
100.00\% / 99.60\% / 100.00\%: \textbf{YES})---validating targets, batching,
and losses, and isolating the failure to the graph model's message
passing/readout. A PD-native port transformer without positions still fails
(job 23801341: \textbf{NO}); adding deterministic first-encounter PD traversal
positions closes all but 3 policy and 8 value states in a four-way sweep (job
23987732), and the tuned configuration (width-128, state residual, traversal
positions, cosine schedule, no dropout; job 23989965) solves the table at epoch
512 in under three minutes: 2,059/2,059 optimal top-policy actions at 99.70\%
mean optimal mass with 2,059/2,059 correct value signs (\textbf{YES}).

\subsection{Shared prime catalog (3--8 crossings)}
\label{sec:shared}

The mixed-size sweep (job 24128608; 35 Spherogram diagrams, 149,319
exact-supervised nonterminal states, seed~0) reaches \textbf{YES} for all three
architectures with per-knot \texttt{SOLVED} lines for every catalog member: the
width-192 six-layer model at epoch~190, width-256 six-layer at epoch~250, and
width-256 eight-layer at epoch~230, each 149,319/149,319 on policy and value
sign in 7~hours 56~minutes total. The width-192 checkpoint is the primary
representation-analysis model as the smallest and earliest solver. Optimization
variance is now partly measured: seed~1 (job 26332106, 2~hours 18~minutes)
solves at epoch~230 with identical final metrics (149,319/149,319 on policy at
99.99\% mean optimal mass and 149,319/149,319 on value sign; 35/35 per-knot
\texttt{SOLVED: YES}), against seed~0's epoch~190. Seed~2 is queued; the G1
three-seed gate closes when it reports.

\subsection{Validation status}
\label{sec:validation}

Structural plus determinism validation covers all $21 \times 256$
eight-crossing terminals: label multiplicities, cyclic under-strand
adjacency, well-defined signs, Jones determinism, and invariance under
canonical relabeling (\texttt{validate\_8crossing\_terminals.py}). The full
55-test suite, the eight-crossing validation (5,376 terminals), and the
35-record manifest leakage check all pass on the current worktree (Bouchet job
26332105, under two minutes). Beyond the production bound, all 49 KnotInfo
nine-crossing table diagrams pass the same structural plus determinism battery
over $49 \times 512 = 25{,}088$ terminals (local validation, 1,051~seconds).
Still required before raising
\texttt{max\_validated\_crossings} to eight or claiming production readiness:
the independent SnapPy/KnotInfo invariant comparison, a short two-GPU SLURM
smoke confirming DDP checkpoint synchronization on current code, and separate
CPU/GPU/memory profiling of self-play, training, and arena phases.
