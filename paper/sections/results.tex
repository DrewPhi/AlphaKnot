\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Fixed seven-crossing shadow}
\label{sec:seven}

The production shadow (the single seven-crossing PD code in
\texttt{config.py}) admits an instructive failure-then-success arc. End-to-end
AlphaZero self-play (job 23623670; 20 iterations, 64 episodes each, 50 MCTS
simulations) reaches only 71.73\% optimal top-policy actions, 71.52\% mean
optimal mass, and 65.27\% value-sign accuracy: certified \textbf{NO}. Exact
supervision of the original graph transformer (job 23748144) stalls at nearly
identical numbers (71.49\% / 71.44\% / 65.42\%: \textbf{NO}), showing the
bottleneck is representational rather than a self-play defect. Ordered-PD
concatenation (job 23760015) and explicit crossing-state attachment (job
23766423) each remain at \textbf{NO}, while a width-256 MLP over the seven
categorical crossing states solves the table in 38~seconds (job 23775537:
100.00\% / 99.60\% / 100.00\%: \textbf{YES})---validating targets, batching,
and losses, and isolating the failure to the graph model's message
passing/readout. A PD-native port transformer without positions still fails
(job 23801341: \textbf{NO}); adding deterministic first-encounter PD traversal
positions closes all but 3 policy and 8 value states in a four-way sweep (job
23987732), and the tuned configuration (width-128, state residual, traversal
positions, cosine schedule, no dropout; job 23989965) solves the table at epoch
512 in under three minutes: 2,059/2,059 optimal top-policy actions at 99.70\%
mean optimal mass with 2,059/2,059 correct value signs (\textbf{YES}).

\subsection{Shared prime catalog (3--8 crossings)}
\label{sec:shared}

The mixed-size sweep (job 24128608; 35 Spherogram diagrams, 149,319
exact-supervised nonterminal states, seed~0) reaches \textbf{YES} for all three
architectures with per-knot \texttt{SOLVED} lines for every catalog member: the
width-192 six-layer model at epoch~190, width-256 six-layer at epoch~250, and
width-256 eight-layer at epoch~230, each 149,319/149,319 on policy and value
sign in 7~hours 56~minutes total. The width-192 checkpoint is the primary
representation-analysis model as the smallest and earliest solver. Optimization
variance is now measured across three seeds of the width-192 configuration
(jobs 24128608/26332106/26332107): all solve with identical final metrics
(149,319/149,319 on policy at 99.99\% mean optimal mass and 149,319/149,319 on
value sign; 35/35 per-knot \texttt{SOLVED: YES} each), with solving epochs
190, 230, and 240 at 2--2.5~hours per seed. The G1 three-seed gate is closed
for this configuration.

\subsection{Train-to-test generalization}
\label{sec:generalization}

Fitting the same width-192 configuration on the frozen \texttt{train} split
only (25 knots, 106,495 states) while grading the disjoint \texttt{test} split
(5 knots, 21,185 states; exact labels grade but never train; checkpoints
selected on train metrics) gives a clean capacity-versus-transfer split
(jobs 26384918/26384919/26384920, seeds 0/1/2, 1.7--1.9~hours each). All three
seeds recover full train mastery---\texttt{MIXED\_SIZE\_CAPACITY\_SOLVED: YES}
at epochs 235, 245, and 235 with every train knot \texttt{SOLVED: YES}---and
all three fail every held-out knot: 15/15 \texttt{HELDOUT~\ldots~SOLVED: NO}
cells. Best-checkpoint held-out agreement is 72.00\% / 67.82\% (seed~0),
70.39\% / 67.81\% (seed~1), and 70.90\% / 65.82\% (seed~2) on policy /
value sign over 21,185 states, with no knot solved by any seed
(e.g.\ $8_{20}$ policy: 4,452, 4,272, and 4,202 of 6,305). Supervised
exact-table training at this scale memorizes the diagrams it sees and does
not transfer to held-out knot types: far above chance, far below the
certification bar, consistently across seeds. This negative result is the
baseline the corpus extension (exact supervision through ten crossings) and
the self-play program must beat; past the exact-table ceiling, certification
gives way to empirical agreement (arena results and raw-versus-MCTS policy
convergence) by design.

\subsection{Nine-crossing capacity frontier}
\label{sec:nine}

The corpus now holds 249 Spherogram table diagrams through ten crossings
(\texttt{prime-catalog-v2}). The first 3--9 capacity probe (job 26414849;
all 84 diagrams, 1,088,698 exact-supervised states, width-192, batch 512,
400-epoch budget) timed out after 18~hours without solving; grading its best
checkpoint (job 26646723, \texttt{--eval-only}) gives 69/84 knots
\texttt{SOLVED: YES} with every one of the 15 failures 1--4 states short of
perfect (combined 99.9976\% policy, 99.9988\% value sign). Two eight-crossing
knots ($8_{17}$, $8_{20}$) regressed two states from their solved 3--8
verdicts---capacity interference from the nine-crossing load. The frontier is
therefore optimization-or-capacity at fixed width, not a collapse; a width-256
probe under the same protocol is the immediate next run.

\subsection{Validation status}
\label{sec:validation}

Structural plus determinism validation covers all $21 \times 256$
eight-crossing terminals: label multiplicities, cyclic under-strand
adjacency, well-defined signs, Jones determinism, and invariance under
canonical relabeling (\texttt{validate\_8crossing\_terminals.py}). The full
55-test suite, the eight-crossing validation (5,376 terminals), and the
35-record manifest leakage check all pass on the current worktree (Bouchet job
26332105, under two minutes). Beyond the production bound, all 49 KnotInfo
nine-crossing table diagrams pass the same structural plus determinism battery
over $49 \times 512 = 25{,}088$ terminals (local validation, 1,051~seconds).
The independent comparison is concretely blocked, not merely pending:
SnapPy~3.3.2's \texttt{jones\_polynomial} raises \texttt{SageNotAvailable} in
the Bouchet environment (verified September~2026), so G2 gate~2 awaits a Sage
installation, a Sage-free Jones implementation, or a mirror-aware KnotInfo
polynomial-table comparison---KnotInfo table hands already differ from corpus
hands (e.g.\ K3a1 is the mirror of \texttt{3\_1}). Still required before raising
\texttt{max\_validated\_crossings} to eight or claiming production readiness:
the independent SnapPy/KnotInfo invariant comparison, a short two-GPU SLURM
smoke confirming DDP checkpoint synchronization on current code, and separate
CPU/GPU/memory profiling of self-play, training, and arena phases.
