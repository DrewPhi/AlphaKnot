\section{Strategy representations and diffusion geometry}
\label{sec:representations}

The network is a game-playing device and, we hypothesize, a strategy
microscope. Once a competent policy/value network exists, its hidden
representations of knot positions can be embedded, clustered by strategic
similarity, and compared against classical invariants---potentially surfacing
motifs that become conjectures. The protocol below
(\texttt{docs/STRATEGY\_EMBEDDINGS.md}) is the preregistered design; the pilot
is exploratory until its controls run.

\subsection{Probe panels and operators}
\label{sec:probes}

A datapoint is a nonterminal partial resolution state indexed in canonical PD
traversal order, so the same ternary pattern means the same probe state on
every same-size shadow. Panels are fixed before evaluation and independent of
any agent's visitation: all 2,059 nonterminal patterns at seven crossings, all
6,305 at eight, and the versioned cross-size pilot panel
\texttt{repeat-all-n3-nonterminal-v1} (19 matched states per knot, three
coordinates repeated periodically through traversal order). Activations are
exported at five stages---post-message-passing port tokens, crossing tokens,
graph token, and penultimate policy/value representations---and each stage
yields a separate diffusion operator via pairwise distances, a versioned
self-tuning Gaussian kernel (fifth-neighbor bandwidth in the pilot) with row
normalization, and optional depth stratification. A parallel MCTS behavioral
operator from full legal-action vectors (prior $p$, visits $\pi$, counts $N$,
values $Q$, network $v$) tests whether internal geometry tracks raw or
search-improved strategy, with $\mathrm{KL}(\pi_{\mathrm{MCTS}}\,\|\,p)$
measuring search correction at fixed budget.

\subsection{Pilot observation}
\label{sec:pilot}

From the solved width-192 checkpoint
(\texttt{c28da11b\ldots}, job 24228941): 35 activation matrices ($19 \times
192$), 35 diffusion operators ($19 \times 19$), and operator-distance plus
665-state PHATE maps~\cite{moon2019visualizing} (automatic diffusion times 11
and 19) computed in 4~minutes 13~seconds. The pilot plot organizes strongly by
crossing count: 31 of 35 knots have a same-crossing-number nearest neighbor in
the two-dimensional embedding. This statistic is descriptive, uncorrected for
class imbalance across crossing numbers, and computed on the visualization
rather than the operator distances---exploratory evidence only.

\subsection{Controls}
\label{sec:controls}

No strategic-geometry claim may be made until: within-crossing full panels
(2,059-state $n{=}7$, 6,305-state $n{=}8$) replace the 19-state pilot;
operators are compared quantitatively, not via 2-D neighborhoods; at least
three training seeds establish stability; untrained, topology-only, solved-MLP,
randomized-policy, relabeling/permutation, multi-diagram, and held-out-split
controls run; invariant enrichment uses held-out prediction with
reward-circular (Jones-adjacent) invariants deprioritized. The manifest
records checkpoint and panel hashes, representation versions, kernel and PHATE
settings, and package versions for every map.
